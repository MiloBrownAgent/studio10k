# DNS Setup for studio10k.com — Email with Google Workspace

Add these records in GoDaddy DNS for studio10k.com. This sets up alex@studio10k.com via Google Workspace.

---

## Step 1: MX Records (email routing — Google Workspace)

Delete any existing MX records first (including any Zoho or ImprovMX records), then add all five:

| Type | Name | Value | Priority |
|------|------|-------|----------|
| MX | @ | ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM | 1 |
| MX | @ | ALT1.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM | 5 |
| MX | @ | ALT2.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM | 5 |
| MX | @ | ALT3.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM | 10 |
| MX | @ | ALT4.ASPMX.L.GOOGLE.COM | 10 |

---

## Step 2: SPF Record (authorizes Google Workspace to send as @studio10k.com)

| Type | Name | Value | TTL |
|------|------|-------|-----|
| TXT | @ | v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all | 1 hour |

If you already have an SPF record, add `include:_spf.google.com` to the existing one rather than creating a second TXT record. There can only be one SPF record per domain.

---

## Step 3: DKIM Record (authenticates outgoing mail — generated by Google)

You'll get this value from Google Workspace Admin Console after adding the domain.

Go to: Google Workspace Admin > Apps > Gmail > Authenticate email > Generate new record

Google will give you a TXT record like this:

| Type | Name | Value | TTL |
|------|------|-------|-----|
| TXT | google._domainkey | v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=**[generated by Google — paste here]** | 1 hour |

Come back and add this once Google generates it.

---

## Step 4: DMARC Record (policy — tells receiving servers what to do with unauthenticated mail)

| Type | Name | Value | TTL |
|------|------|-------|-----|
| TXT | _dmarc | v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:alex@studio10k.com | 1 hour |

Start with `p=none` (monitor only). Once you confirm SPF and DKIM are working cleanly, upgrade to `p=quarantine` or `p=reject`.

---

## Summary Checklist

- [ ] Delete old MX records (Zoho/ImprovMX)
- [ ] Add 5 Google MX records
- [ ] Add SPF TXT record
- [ ] Add DMARC TXT record
- [ ] Set up Google Workspace, add domain, generate DKIM key
- [ ] Add DKIM TXT record (after Google generates it)
- [ ] Verify all records in Google Workspace Admin

DNS changes typically propagate within 15 minutes to 1 hour on GoDaddy. Google Workspace may take up to 48 hours to fully verify.
